TRANSPARENCY CORNER

Receipts over vibes.

Listening minutes are summed from Spotify's recorded msPlayed values for plays dated Jan 1–Dec 31, 2026. Rankings use recorded play events, not Spotify's proprietary Wrapped algorithm. Genre and track popularity are not reliably included in streaming-history exports, so those awards stay locked until we have a trustworthy metadata source.
