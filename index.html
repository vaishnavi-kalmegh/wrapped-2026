Bring the evidence.

Choose one or more Spotify Extended Streaming History JSON files. They're analyzed in this browser and never uploaded by this app.

How to download your Spotify listening history
  1. Open Spotify’s Account Privacy page and sign in.
  2. Find Download your data and request Extended streaming history.
  3. Confirm the request if Spotify emails you, then wait for Spotify to prepare the archive. It can take up to 30 days.
  4. Extract the archive on your computer and select the listening-history .json files below. Multiple files are supported.

Privacy: Never share your raw export publicly. This page reads files in your browser; it does not send them to a server.

TRANSPARENCY CORNER

Receipts over vibes.

Listening time is the sum of recorded msPlayed / ms_played values for dated track records from Jan 1 through Nov 15, 2026 (UTC; approximate Wrapped cutoff). Top songs count records with more than 30,000 ms played. Artist ranking approximates Spotify's published primary/featured weighting: primary artist = 1.0 point and explicitly listed secondary/featured artists = 0.5 point. Spotify does not publish its exact weights, and many exports do not identify featured artists separately, so artist ranks are estimates—not an exact reproduction of Spotify Wrapped. This export may not expose Private Mode, Taste Profile exclusions, or reliable content-type filtering; the app cannot reproduce those rules. Genre and track popularity are not fabricated.

Story cards are generated locally. No accounts, server, analytics, or data persistence are used by this static app. Unofficial fan-made project, not affiliated with Spotify.
